About the resource library

Prompts, instructions and skills all give an AI directions. Choose what you want to reuse:

  • Prompts: Messages that ask an AI to complete a task.
  • Instructions: Rules for how an AI works or responds.
  • Skills: Reusable task instructions packaged for an AI app that supports skills.

Open a resource to see its purpose, expected outcomes, requirements and exact usage steps.
