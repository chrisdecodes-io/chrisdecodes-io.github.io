Task context starter prompt

Purpose

Give an AI assistant your goal, relevant context and constraints before it starts a task.

Works with
Any AI assistant that accepts text prompts
Format
Plain text (.txt)

Expected outcomes

  • A task brief that brings your goal, relevant context, constraints and preferred output together.
  • A request for the assistant to clarify missing information that could materially change its answer.

How to use it

  1. Copy the prompt or download the text file.
  2. Replace every bracketed field with information about your task.
  3. Paste it into your AI assistant, then review and refine the answer.

What you need

  • An AI assistant that accepts text input.
  • Your goal, relevant background, constraints and preferred output format.

The prompt

I want to [specific goal].
Here’s the relevant context: [background].
My constraints are [time, tools, or limits].
A useful result would look like [format or example].
Ask me about any missing information that would materially change your answer.
Download .txtView source

Things to know

  • This prompt organizes your task brief. It does not verify facts or guarantee a correct answer; review the assistant’s response before using it.

Keep private information out unless you understand and accept how your chosen tool handles it.
